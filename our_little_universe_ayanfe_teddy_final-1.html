A letter from my heart

To my Ayanfe, on your birthday

If I could gather every little feeling, every prayer, and every thing I appreciate about you, I think it might look a little like this.

My Ayanfe, my love,

Happy birthday, Ayanfe. 💜

I have been trying to find the right words for you, and the truth is that I don't think words will ever be enough to explain every feeling in my heart. But I still want to try, because you deserve to be celebrated with intention. I want you to have something you can come back to—not just today, but on an ordinary day when you need a reminder that you are loved, appreciated, and genuinely rooted for.

We have known each other for only a few months, and I won't pretend that I know every part of you already. We are still learning each other, still discovering the little things that make us who we are. But even in this beginning, there are things about you that have touched my heart in ways I did not expect. I am grateful that I get to know you one conversation, one memory, one honest moment at a time.

One thing I appreciate about our story is that I did not enter this relationship without thinking carefully about my walk with God. I was trying to grow in my relationship with Christ, and I did not want a relationship to distract me from Him. That mattered to me then, and it still matters to me now. I want what we are building to be something we handle with care, wisdom, patience, and prayer. I want us to encourage each other toward God, not away from Him.

Ayanfe, you have a way of making me feel seen. Not just noticed for a moment, but listened to. You remember little things I say. You pay attention to what makes me happy. You check in on me, and sometimes you give me the encouragement I need at exactly the moment I need it. The little things might look ordinary from the outside, but they don't feel ordinary to me. They stay with me.

I still think about how you remembered my casual “don't let the bugs bite” goodbye and said it back to me later. It was such a small thing, but that is part of what made it special. You had listened. You had kept it somewhere in your mind. And you gave it back to me in a way that made me feel that my words—and the little moments we share—matter to you.

I think about our first video call, too, when I fell asleep and you stayed on the call until I woke up. It is one of those memories that makes me smile because it says something about the tenderness in the little moments. Sometimes care isn't a grand speech or a big plan. Sometimes it is simply choosing to stay present, being patient, and letting an ordinary moment become a memory worth keeping.

And then there is your letter. Ayanfe, you were the first guy to write me something so long and thoughtful that I could honestly describe it as a novel. I spent over two hours reading it. Two whole hours with your words, taking them in, feeling one emotion after another. I cried so much that my nose started running. 😭 I was overwhelmed—not just by the length, but by the thought behind it. It made me feel loved, seen, special, and so many things I still find difficult to put into words. I felt the care you had taken to express yourself, and I will not forget how that felt. May God bless you for me. Truly.

And today, when we talked about my business and I was feeling bad about some mistakes I had made, you encouraged me. You gave me the kind of words I needed to hear. You reminded me, in your own way, that one mistake does not have to be the end of the story. I appreciate that you root for me. I appreciate that you want to see me succeed. I appreciate that you can meet me in a moment when I feel disappointed in myself and help me remember that I can keep learning and growing.

I want you to know that I am rooting for you, too. I want to see you become all that God has placed in your heart to become. I want to celebrate your wins, encourage you through the difficult parts, and remind you that your efforts matter even when progress feels slow. Your dreams matter. Your peace matters. Your growth matters. You deserve to have people around you who genuinely want to see you thrive, and I pray I can be one of those people for you.

And please, let's talk about the little hand-love picture. 😭💜 You did it and sent it to me, and now it is my wallpaper. It makes me happy, and I love that something so small has become something I get to see on my phone every day. Maybe one day it will look like such a tiny thing compared with all the memories we will have made, but right now it is part of our beginning, and I want to remember it just as it is.

I don't want to rush ahead and pretend I know every chapter of our story. We have only just begun learning each other. There are still so many conversations to have, things to understand, new memories to make, and ways to grow. But I am thankful for what I have experienced so far. I am thankful for your thoughtfulness, your encouragement, your effort, and the happiness that comes from sharing these little moments with you.

Above everything, my prayer is that Christ remains at the centre of our lives and whatever we are building together. May we never become so focused on each other that we forget the One who gives us wisdom, grace, and love. May we learn to communicate honestly, listen patiently, apologise when we are wrong, forgive with understanding, and treat each other with gentleness. May we encourage each other's faith and keep making room for God to shape us as individuals and together.

On your birthday, I pray that God blesses you beyond what you can see right now. May He give you wisdom for the decisions ahead, strength for the days that test you, peace when life feels uncertain, and courage to keep working toward the things He has placed on your heart. May He bless the work of your hands, establish your good plans, open the right doors, and give you discernment to recognise the opportunities that are right for you. May your efforts bear good fruit, and may you have the patience to keep going while you grow.

May God protect you wherever you go. May He surround you with people who are sincere, strengthen your relationship with Him, and remind you that your worth is not measured only by what you achieve. I pray that this new year of your life brings meaningful growth, genuine joy, good health, clarity, and beautiful reasons to give thanks. May you always have the courage to dream and the humility to keep learning.

And when life gets busy or difficult, I hope you remember that you do not have to have everything figured out all at once. You are allowed to learn. You are allowed to take things one step at a time. I hope you give yourself some of the patience and encouragement you so readily give to me.

Thank you for the little things, Ayanfe. Thank you for remembering, for checking in, for encouraging me, for making an effort, and for letting me experience these moments with you. Thank you for the letter that made me cry, the conversations that stretch through the day, and the small gestures that have found their way into my heart. I may not always know how to explain how much something means to me, but I hope you never mistake my words being insufficient for my appreciation being small.

Happy birthday, my Ayanfe. May this next chapter of your life be full of God's goodness, purposeful growth, answered prayers, and moments that remind you how precious life is. I pray you become more confident in the person God is shaping you to be, and that you never lose the kindness and thoughtfulness that make these early memories so special to me.

Here is to more conversations, more laughter, more little things to remember, more encouragement, more learning, and more growing in grace. One day at a time, one memory at a time, with God at the centre.

With so much love and prayers,

Your Teddy. 💜
